# KPI definitions
Revenue is quantity times unit price after line discount, in EGP, before returns. Returns are tracked separately and are not netted out of revenue.
Profit (gross profit) is revenue minus quantity times unit cost. Margin means profit divided by revenue.
Average order value (AOV) is revenue divided by the number of distinct orders.
Active customer means a customer with at least one order in the period.
Dates use the calendar month of the order date. The latest complete month in the data is October 2026.
Orders is the count of distinct order IDs; one order can contain several product lines.
